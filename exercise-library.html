Bodyweight Squat

✕
BeginnerLegsBodyweight

Description

A squat you can do without added weight.

Targeted muscles

Primary: Legs. Secondary: Glutes, Core.

Steps

  1. Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart.
  2. Bend your knees and hips to lower as far as you can control.
  3. Press through your feet to stand tall.

Proper form

Keep your knees tracking in the direction of your toes.

Suggested sets and reps

2–3 × 8–12

✕
BeginnerChestBench

Description

An elevated push-up that reduces the load on the arms.

Targeted muscles

Primary: Chest. Secondary: Shoulders, Triceps.

Steps

  1. Set your hands on a stable bench and step back.
  2. Lower your chest toward the bench without letting your hips sag.
  3. Press back to the starting position.

Proper form

Keep your head, hips, and heels in a straight line.

Suggested sets and reps

2–3 × 8–12

Description

A floor exercise that trains hip extension.

Targeted muscles

Primary: Legs. Secondary: Glutes, Hamstrings, Core.

Steps

  1. Lie down with knees bent and feet flat.

Reverse Lunge

✕
BeginnerLegsBodyweight

Description

A step-back lunge that trains one leg at a time.

Targeted muscles

Primary: Legs. Secondary: Glutes, Core.

Steps

  1. Stand tall and step one foot back.
  2. Bend both knees while keeping the front foot planted.
  3. Push through the front foot to stand, then switch sides.

Proper form

Keep your front knee pointed in the same direction as your toes.

Suggested sets and reps

2–3 × 8 per side

Push-up

✕
BeginnerChestBodyweight

Description

A bodyweight press that builds the chest and arms.

Targeted muscles

Primary: Chest. Secondary: Shoulders, triceps, core.

Equipment

None

Steps

  1. Place hands slightly wider than your shoulders, body in a straight line.
  2. Lower your chest toward the floor with elbows about 45° from your body.
  3. Push back up without letting your hips sag.

Proper form

Keep your core tight and your neck relaxed.

Common mistake

Sagging hips or flaring elbows wide.

Suggested sets and reps

3 × 8–12

Safety: Start with a manageable resistance, move with control, breathe steadily, and stop if you feel sharp pain. Seek qualified guidance for injuries or medical restrictions.

Dead Bug

✕
BeginnerCoreBodyweight

Description

A slow core exercise that protects the lower back.

Targeted muscles

Primary: Core. Secondary: Hip flexors.

Equipment

None

Steps

  1. Lie on your back, arms up, knees bent at 90°.
  2. Lower the opposite arm and leg slowly, back flat.
  3. Return and switch sides.

Proper form

Press your lower back into the floor.

Common mistake

Letting the lower back lift off the floor.

Suggested sets and reps

3 × 8 each side
